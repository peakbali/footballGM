## Full description (max 4000)

Take a tiny club from the ninth tier of the football pyramid all the way to the top division.

Peak Football is a fast, offline football management game. You are the general manager: sign the players, pick the formation, hire the staff, build the ground and balance the books. Matches are played in a single tap, so a whole season fits into a coffee break.

THE CLIMB
- A ten-division pyramid with more than 1,000 clubs and over 20,000 players, all fictional
- Promotion, relegation and playoffs at every level
- A national knockout cup where small clubs can meet the giants
- A four-week pre-season to get your squad ready before every campaign

BUILD YOUR SQUAD
- Scout, shortlist and bid for players, and negotiate with clubs
- Contracts, loans, free agents and sell-on clauses
- Youth academy prospects who might grow into stars
- Players develop with age, form and their own potential

RUN THE CLUB
- Upgrade the training ground, academy, stands and more
- Sponsors, ticket prices, finances and a board with goals each season
- Decisions through the year: transfer requests, offers, player life events
- Design your own club name, colours and crest

YOUR LEGACY
- A Hall of Fame with all-time club records and a ledger for every player
- An end-of-season review of your highs and lows
- 100 achievements to unlock

MADE FOR PHONES
- Plays fully offline, no account needed
- Five save slots
- Pixel art and relaxed lo-fi music

FAIR PRICING
The first season of every career is free. If you enjoy it, one purchase unlocks every season after that, for every save slot. No ads, no subscriptions, no energy timers, no loot boxes.

All clubs, leagues, stadiums and players in Peak Football are fictional.
