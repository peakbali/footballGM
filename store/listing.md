## Full description (max 4000)

Take a tiny club from the ninth tier of the football pyramid all the way to the Premier Division.

FootballGM is a fast, offline football management game. You are the general manager: you sign the players, set the formation, hire the staff, build the ground and balance the books. Matches are played in a single tap, so a whole season fits into a coffee break.

THE CLIMB
- A ten-division pyramid with more than 1,000 clubs and 24,000 players, all fictional
- Promotion, relegation and playoffs at every level
- A national knockout cup where non-league minnows can meet the giants

BUILD YOUR SQUAD
- Scout, shortlist and bid for players; haggle with clubs and agents
- Contracts, loans, free agents and sell-on clauses
- Youth academy prospects who grow into stars (or never quite make it)
- Every player develops with age, form and their own potential

RUN THE CLUB
- Facilities: training ground, academy, stands, commercial and more
- Sponsors, finances and a board with goals each season
- Decisions that pop up through the year: transfer requests, life events, offers you can't refuse
- Design your own club name, colours and crest

MADE FOR PHONES
- Plays fully offline, no account needed
- Five save slots and 100 achievements
- Pixel art, relaxed lo-fi music, short sessions

FAIR PRICING
The first season of every career is free. If you enjoy it, one purchase unlocks every season after that, for every save slot. No ads, no subscriptions, no energy timers, no loot boxes.

All clubs, leagues, stadiums and players in FootballGM are fictional.
