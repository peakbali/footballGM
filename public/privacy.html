FootballGM privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

FootballGM is an offline, single-player football management game. It is built to collect as little as possible: there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your saved careers, settings and achievements are stored only on your device, in your browser's or the app's local storage. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

What we do not collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number, contacts or location).
  • No advertising IDs, analytics, crash reporting or tracking cookies.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Purchases

The first season of every career is free. The optional one-time "Full game" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play under the Google privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

After a purchase, the game sends Google's purchase token and the product ID to a small server we run, which asks Google Play to confirm the purchase (Google refunds purchases that are not confirmed). That server does not store the token or anything else, and the token does not identify you to us.

Internet access

The game downloads its own files (code, images, fonts) from GitHub Pages so it can run and update. GitHub may log standard technical information such as IP addresses for those requests, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. After the first load the game plays offline.

Children

FootballGM does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Fiction

All clubs, leagues, stadiums and players in FootballGM are fictional. Any resemblance to real teams or people is a coincidence.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/peakbali/footballGM/issues or use the contact email on the Google Play listing.
